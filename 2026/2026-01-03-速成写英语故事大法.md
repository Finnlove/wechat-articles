之前我们说了可以用介词短语做后置定语或者地点状语，比如A man in an old suit sat on the rock near the tree.那么这里我们交代了什么样的人，在哪里做什么，但是没有说时间，其实在英语中，介词短语也可以用来表示时间。

       大家最常见的介词短语做时间状语，比如on Sunday，before 12 o'clock，这类我们可以理解为方位介词叠甲做地点状语的延伸，只是空间变成了时间，例如He sat down on an enormous rock at seven o'clock this morning.因此我们可以很自然的写出这样的看起来很难很长的句子，实际上这只是一个非常普通的简单句。

再比如，我们来汉译英：他挽起袖子露出胳膊捞，从一个很深的水池里捞出了那个金灿灿的戒指。是不是很难？

He rolled up his sleeves over his elbows and fished the shining golden ring out of the deep pool.

看，其实也不难，先说他做的这两个事，这两个事情是一个主干：他挽袖子子并且捞戒指：

Pull up the sleeves and fish out the ring

捞袖子到什么程度啊？over elbow。然后什么样的戒指啊？shining golden ring。从哪里捞出来啊。out of the pool，什么样的pool？deep pool.

一直到这里，我们都在说他怎么做这个事情的没有提到时间，现在加上时间，特别是当别人做什么的时候，这不就成了从句：

Last evening，when everyone had fallen asleep，放在句首，我们来读一读我们写的故事：

Last evening, when everyone had fallen asleep, he rolled up his sleeves over his elbows and fished the shining golden ring out of the deep pool.

现在我们加上副词，我们用熟悉的quietly slowly carefully再来读读：

Last evening, when everyone had fallen asleep, he slowly rolled up his sleeves over his elbows and carefully fished the shining golden ring out of the deep pool！

看，这不就会写故事了。

  此外我们可以大胆的使用中文中的拟人，比如Those stars twinkled their eyes when they saw everything.

然后我们用上比喻：

The trees waved branches in the wind ，and their shadows looked like ghosts.The man was scared because of those shadows.

我们连起来读一下：

Last evening, when everyone had fallen asleep, he slowly rolled up his sleeves over his elbows and carefully fished the shining golden ring out of the deep pool！

The trees waved their branches in the wind.Their shadows looked like ghosts.The man was scared because of them.Suddenly,a black owl broke the silence.The moonlight covered the whole village.

这不就学会写小作文啦。
